About the Programme

What is DFD?

DFD refers to the Diploma in Computer Technology (Big Data), a programme that develops students' skills in computer technology, data management, data analysis and system development.

Students learn how to collect, organise, process, analyse, visualise and interpret data to support better decision-making in real-world applications.
